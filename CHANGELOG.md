# Changelog

## 0.2.0

First public build.

- One keybind (and a panel button) that targets Dundun and puts a raid marker on him.
- Panel shows only in delves, reports whether the press found him, and pulses a reminder until it does.
- Alert banner and sound the first time he is marked in a run.
- Settings page under Options > AddOns > Dundun Finder: raid icon, alerts, reminder interval, panel position, scale, lock, and hide-once-marked, with a preview button for positioning.
- Slash commands under /dundun for everything on the settings page.
